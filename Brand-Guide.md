# The Home Plans — brand guide v1

Created 25 October 2026. Design proposal for thehomeplans.com; not a live WordPress implementation.

## Position and voice
Space, thoughtfully planned. Practical English-language home-planning guides built around original drawings and clear design reasoning. USA first; meaningful AU/CA context later. Voice: specific, calm and useful. Explain trade-offs; never invent projects, professional credentials, testing or approval guarantees.

## Identity assets
- assets/logo.svg: primary floor-plan symbol and serif wordmark.
- assets/logo-reverse.svg: light version for dark backgrounds.
- assets/mark.svg: compact icon / favicon.
- Clear space: at least half the icon width around the lockup. Minimum full-lockup width: 230 px; use the icon below that.
- Do not stretch, add shadows, rearrange the lettering or recolour arbitrarily.
- SVG wordmark uses live Georgia text; outline the final lettering for production portability. Brand-name/trademark clearance has not been completed.

## Colour tokens
Paper #F7F4EE; forest ink #253D35; olive #596B56; sage wash #E8ECDF; clay #966347. Main reading uses forest ink on paper. Sage is a background, not body text. Clay is for restrained annotations. Maintain readable contrast in future components.

## Type and composition
Georgia headings; Arial body/interface. System-font stack with no external requests. Desktop H1 up to 78 px; mobile around 43–52 px; body 16–18 px; line height about 1.6–1.85. Article reading column about 680 px. Comfortable whitespace, simple rules, quiet backgrounds. Avoid stock-photo mosaics, fake testimonials and decorative animation.

## Diagram rules
Original SVG concept samples supplied. Use dark wall outlines, muted furniture fills and clear dimension annotations. The current bed footprint assumes 60 by 80 inches. It is not a universal size assertion. Label concept status and scale assumptions. A 10 ft side converts exactly to 3.048 m. Plans require Shabaz's checks for geometry, actual frame dimensions, openings and access before publication. Create a distinct 12x12 concept; do not reuse the 10x10 thumbnail as a validated 12x12 design. Client work needs permission.

## Templates supplied
index.html: homepage; article.html: interactive article design; rooms.html: filtered guide library; start-here.html: reader starting sequence; brand.html: visual brand guide.

The six launch topics remain the approved research shortlist. Five guides show an honest planned-content outline; the 10x10 page has sample editorial copy for template review, not a finished expert-reviewed article. Search operates on that planned library. Save uses this browser's local storage. No forms, tracking, ads or email subscriptions are active.

## WordPress handoff
Implement the tokens as reusable global styles, the header/footer as shared template parts, and the article components as reusable patterns. Match the selected research URLs during implementation; these HTML filenames are preview routes only. Curated hubs should own their intended pages without duplicate indexable archives. Keep staging noindex; review production indexing deliberately at launch. Add verified author details and tailored policies. Reserve advertising slots only when actual partners/placements are selected; avoid layout shifts and misleading ad/navigation proximity. Do not add placeholder ad code.

## Remaining work
Shabaz biography and final logo lettering export; original 10x10/12x12 assets and technical review; full researched articles; verified contact details and tailored policies; WordPress build and live platform QA. The design does not itself establish rankings, trust or ad-network eligibility.
