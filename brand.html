01 / Position

Space, thoughtfully planned.

Help homeowners understand layouts and make better space decisions through original drawings, clear explanations and honest trade-offs. USA-first English, with meaningful Australian and Canadian context as the library grows.

02 / Identity

An open floor-plan symbol paired with a serif wordmark. The symbol suggests rooms and movement without pretending to be a certified architectural seal.

Primary SVG ↓Reverse SVG ↓Icon / favicon SVG ↓

Use the icon at small sizes. Keep clear space around the logo; never stretch or add a drop shadow. Wordmarks use live Georgia text and should be outlined before final production export.

03 / Colour

Paper is the canvas. Forest ink carries the reading. Olive adds emphasis, sage groups related ideas, and clay marks annotations. Keep body text dark and backgrounds quiet.

04 / Typography

A better place
to call home.

Georgia for expressive editorial headings. Arial for clear body copy, navigation and diagram labels. This preview uses system fonts and needs no external font request.

Body: 16–18 px with generous line spacing. Reading column: about 680 px. Small labels: 11–12 px. Headings should wrap naturally on mobile.

05 / Diagrams

Use strong wall lines, light furniture fills and a consistent dimension style. Label assumptions and concept status. New 10×10 and 12×12 layouts require Shabaz’s review before publication.

06 / Voice

Explain the decision behind the drawing.

Write in plain English. Be specific, helpful and measured. Show alternatives and limitations. Avoid “perfect for everyone,” invented client stories, guaranteed results and unnecessary jargon.

Example: “Check the door swing before placing the dresser.”

07 / Templates

Homepage: purpose → featured plan → guide library → starting steps → author.
Article: clear question → direct answer → original diagrams → comparison → useful next step.
WordPress: carry these colours, typography, spacing and components into reusable styles and templates.

Keep ads separate from navigation and diagrams. Reserve space when actual placements are chosen; do not crowd the first answer. Logo and visual design do not guarantee ad-network approval.

08 / Before launch

Confirm Shabaz’s public biography, complete expert-reviewed articles and original visuals, add real contact details and tailored policies, and test the WordPress build on mobile. Brand-name/trademark clearance has not been performed.

Practical ideas. Clear drawings. More thoughtful spaces.
